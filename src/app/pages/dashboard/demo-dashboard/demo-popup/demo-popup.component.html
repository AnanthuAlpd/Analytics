dashboard Welcome to PRABHA

This AI Enabled BI Tool PRABHA provides an interactive sales prediction and analysis dashboard for a textile shop, leveraging 5 years of historical sales data.

star Key Features

1. Sales Prediction using Historical Data

  • A line graph visualizes the predicted sales trends based on 5 years of past sales data.
  • Machine learning or statistical models are applied to forecast future demand.

2. Product-wise Sales Analysis (2022 vs. 2023)

  • A bar chart compares sales of textile products between 2022 and 2023.
  • Helps identify top-performing products and declining trends.

3. Month-wise Revenue Comparison

  • A bar chart displays monthly revenue across different years.
  • Enables seasonal trend analysis.

star Business Benefits

  • Accurate Demand Forecasting – Reduces overstocking/understocking.
  • Performance Tracking – Identifies best-selling products.
  • Data-Driven Decisions – Optimizes pricing and inventory.

This dashboard empowers textile shop owners with actionable insights, improving profitability through data-driven strategies. Future enhancements could include real-time sales tracking and AI-powered recommendations.
